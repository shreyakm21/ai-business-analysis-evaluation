# Evaluation Methodology

## Purpose

Evaluate how four freely accessed AI assistants handle
defined retail business tasks and identify checks needed
before their outputs are used.

All business materials are synthetic. Separate cases may
contain different figures and must not be combined.

## Test design

- Tools: ChatGPT, Claude, Microsoft Copilot, and Gemini.
- Access: accounts reported by the evaluator as free.
- Each tool received identical source material and prompts
  within each case.
- Completed benchmark cases used fresh chats.
- Each benchmark case was tested once per tool.
- Source files were supplied without answer keys.
- Original outputs were preserved, including mistakes.

The P01 sales pilot used three stages in the same
conversation: initial analysis, follow-up, and verification.
These are not independent repeat runs.

Model names were user-reported and not independently verified.
Results apply to the observed sessions, not all versions
or subscription plans.

## Reference answers

Numerical reference answers for the sales pilot were
calculated and cross-checked in Excel.

For benchmark cases, answer keys and acceptance checks
were established before reviewing tool responses, using
the supplied policies, transcripts, and reports.

AI assistance supported preparation and review.
The evaluator maintained the workbook and reviewed the
AI-assisted assessments.

## Scoring

Dimensions are scored separately from 0 to 3:

| Dimension | What is assessed |
|---|---|
| Final answer accuracy | Correct requested results and conclusions |
| Supporting accuracy | Correct displayed calculations and factual claims |
| Requirement completion | Coverage of requested outputs and constraints |
| Evidence boundaries | Appropriate handling of uncertainty and missing evidence |
| Useful depth | Relevant explanation that helps the business user |

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 3 | Meets the criterion; no issue identified |
| 2 | Minor issue that does not change the business interpretation |
| 1 | Material issue or substantial omission requiring correction |
| 0 | Fundamentally incorrect, unusable, or fails the criterion |

Presentation was assessed informally during the pilot.
It was not assessed for the completed benchmark cases.

No combined score or overall tool ranking is calculated.
Visible calculations and explanations are assessed;
hidden internal reasoning is not.

## Error severity and approval

Minor errors do not change the business interpretation.

Material errors could change a conclusion, recommendation,
commitment, or decision.

A response with an unresolved material error is not ready
for decision use, regardless of its other scores.

Review records distinguish:
- Ready for human approval
- Needs revision
- Unable to assess

Readiness is a proposed project judgment, not authorization
to use an output in a real business.

## Evidence preservation

Original answers and later corrections are separate records.
Corrections do not overwrite initial failures.

Copied text may lose interface formatting. Suspected copying
artifacts are flagged rather than automatically attributed
to the model.

## Timing

Pilot response completion time was measured from submission
until the complete answer finished displaying.

It excludes file upload, human review, and correction effort.
Later benchmark timings were not consistently recorded.

No controlled manual baseline or productivity-saving
estimate has been established.

## Limitations

The evaluation uses small synthetic cases and one benchmark
attempt per tool per case. It does not measure repeatability
or population-level error rates.

Scoring is AI-assisted, reviewed by one evaluator, and
not blinded. No independent second reviewer was used.

Findings support case-specific observations and proposed
controls, not universal rankings, certification, or claims
of enterprise suitability.
