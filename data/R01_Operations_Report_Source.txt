SYNTHETIC BUSINESS MATERIAL — MAPLE & MAIN
Case ID: R01
October 2026 Operations Report
Prepared: 2 November 2026
All monetary amounts are INR.

1. SALES PERFORMANCE

Net sales were INR 96,000 in October, compared with
INR 90,000 in September.

October's net-sales target was INR 100,000.

October Online net sales were INR 52,000.
October Store net sales were INR 44,000.

Product costs and operating expenses are not included.
Channel profitability cannot be calculated from this report.

2. DELIVERY PERFORMANCE

The delivery dashboard reports 180 on-time deliveries
out of 200 completed deliveries in October.

The on-time delivery target was 95%.

A separate narrative update from the delivery team states:
"We achieved 94% on-time delivery in October."

The two sources use the same stated reporting period.
The reason for the difference has not been investigated.
Neither source has been designated authoritative.

3. RETURNS

October recorded 24 returned units from 300 units sold.
September recorded 18 returned units from 300 units sold.

The team suspects packaging damage contributed to the
increase, but return reasons have not yet been validated.

4. CUSTOMER FEEDBACK

An optional October survey received 20 responses.
Sixteen respondents rated their experience positively.

The survey was sent to 200 customers.
Respondents were self-selected.

The survey does not establish the satisfaction level
of all customers.
