# Maple & Main — October 2026 Operations Briefing

Case R01 · Prepared 2 November 2026 · All monetary amounts in INR

## 1. Executive summary

October net sales increased 6.67% compared with September but fell short of the October target by INR 4,000. Delivery performance is disputed: the dashboard indicates 90%, while the delivery narrative reports 94%; neither is designated authoritative. The return rate increased by 2 percentage points. Customer feedback was positive among survey respondents, but the optional, self-selected survey does not establish overall customer satisfaction.

## 3. Data-quality issues and limitations

- Delivery figures conflict: dashboard 90% versus narrative 94%, for the same period. The authoritative source and cause remain unconfirmed.
- Packaging damage is a suspected contributor to increased returns, not a validated cause.
- Survey respondents were self-selected; the 80% positive result applies only to respondents, not all customers.
- Product costs and operating expenses are absent. Channel profitability cannot be calculated.
- The report does not establish that proposed packaging or promotional changes would improve outcomes.
