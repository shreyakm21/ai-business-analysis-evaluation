### 3. Data-Quality Issues and Limitations

* **Profitability:** Product costs and operating expenses are excluded; channel profitability cannot be calculated.


* **Conflicting Delivery Data:** The delivery dashboard reports 90.00% on-time (180 out of 200), whereas a narrative update reports 94.00%. Neither source is designated authoritative, and the reason for the difference is uninvestigated.


* **Survey Limitations:** The customer feedback survey reflects a self-selected sample of 20 respondents out of 200 surveyed and does not establish the satisfaction level of all customers.


* **Unvalidated Returns:** Returned units increased from 18 to 24 month-over-month. While packaging damage is suspected by the team, return reasons remain unvalidated.
