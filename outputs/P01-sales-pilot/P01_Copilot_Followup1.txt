4. Can We Conclude Marketing Caused the Increase?

No.

The dataset demonstrates that net sales rose from INR 27,700.00 in August 2026 to INR 33,250.00 in September 2026, but it contains no marketing variables or causal evidence linking marketing activity to that increase.

At most, the data supports analysis of sales, returns, discounts, channels, and product mix. It does not support a conclusion that marketing caused the September-over-August increase.

Limitations

The dataset does not include:

Marketing activity or spend.
Product costs or profit margins.
Customer-level information.
Inventory levels.
Traffic or conversion metrics.
Any causal experiment or control group.

Consequently, findings are descriptive rather than causal.
