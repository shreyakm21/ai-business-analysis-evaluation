### Clarifications to the previous response

I would make three refinements:

1\. Discounts: Explicitly identify the weighted discount rate as weighted by units sold, rather than implying it is a sales-weighted discount measure.

2\. Product mix: Describe the results as product-level net-sales changes, not as a fully isolated product-mix effect.

3\. External causes: State more explicitly that the dataset can quantify changes in units, returns, discounts, and product-level net sales, but cannot determine whether marketing, seasonality, or external demand factors caused those changes.

These are clarifications of interpretation, not corrections to the underlying arithmetic.
