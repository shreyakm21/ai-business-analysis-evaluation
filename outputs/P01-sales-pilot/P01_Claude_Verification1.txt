## 5. Errors and misleading statements

I found no numerical errors in the September vs August analysis. Four smaller points:

1. **Store growth rounding (already corrected).** My first answer gave Store's September growth as +0.76%. The correct figure is +0.77%, as I stated in my second response.
2. **Word counts I quoted were off.** I described my first summary as "about 85 words" when it was 74, and my second as 64 words when it was 67. Both were within their limits (120 and 80).
3. **"Driven by more units sold" needs a caveat.** The Online figures support it as arithmetic, but it should not be read as a cause. The volume vs price/mix split also depends on the order of calculation (I valued volume at August net sales per unit). A different order would give a different split, though the total is the same. The price/mix figure also bundles product mix, list price and discount effects.
4. **Small samples.** Each channel-month has only 3 lines, and the return counts are 1 to 5 units. My earlier limitation notes covered this.

The conclusion that marketing causation is not established stands.

## 6. Corrected management summary (58 words)

Comparing September 2026 with August 2026, net sales rose ₹5,550.00 (+20.04%), from ₹27,700.00 to ₹33,250.00. Online added ₹5,430.00 (+44.80% on its August level; 97.84% of the increase), with more units sold but lower net sales per unit. Store added ₹120.00 (+0.77%). The data cannot show why Online units rose; marketing is untested, and no causal conclusion is supported.
