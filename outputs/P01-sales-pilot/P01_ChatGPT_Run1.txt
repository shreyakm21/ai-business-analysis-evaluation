## 1. Total net sales

Total net sales · July–September 2026

# ₹84,090.00

After discounts and returns, before tax and shipping.

### Calculation method

For each product line:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{Net Sales}={}&(\text{Units Sold}-\text{Units Returned})\\
&\times\text{Unit Price}\\
&\times(1-\text{Discount Rate})
\end{aligned}
\]
For example, the first dataset row (L001) records:

- Units sold: 10
- Units returned: 2
- Unit price: ₹500
- Discount rate: 10%

\[
(10-2)\times500\times(1-0.10)
\]

\[
=8\times500\times0.90
=\text{₹3,600.00}
\]

The calculation is applied separately to all 18 rows, and the resulting net sales are summed:

\\[ \boxed{\text{Total Net Sales}=\text{₹84,090.00}} \\]

This method ensures that returned units do not contribute to net sales, while the discount is applied to every unit retained by the customer.

Month-over-month (MoM) growth is calculated as:

\\[ \text{MoM Growth}= \frac{\text{Current Month Net Sales}-\text{Previous Month Net Sales}} {\text{Previous Month Net Sales}}\times100 \\]

## 6. What the data establishes — and what it cannot answer

- Store generated ₹1,550.00 more net sales.

However, profitability is not equivalent to net sales.

To compare profitability, Maple & Main would need additional information, such as:

| Missing information                    | Why it matters                                      |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |

### b. Did successful marketing cause September's growth?

No. The supplied data does not establish that successful marketing caused September's growth.

The dataset establishes that September net sales increased by:

However, it contains no information about:

Therefore, marketing success is a possible explanation, but it cannot be confirmed from these sales records alone.
